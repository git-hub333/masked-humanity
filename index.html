Why the Wheels Matter

🏍

Reaching communities in need is not always easy. Long distances, tough roads, and limited transport can make humanitarian work slower and more difficult.

With a Honda XR150L, Masked Humanity will have greater mobility to move where the work is needed and carry essential supplies to places that need help most.

These wheels will not just move our team, they will move the mission forward.

About Masked Humanity

Masked Humanity is the persona behind personal humanitarian outreach — helping people quietly, without putting a face or name to it.

This is a personal fundraiser, run by one individual, not a registered charity or organization. Funds are received directly by that individual and used toward the stated goal.

This campaign is one step toward reaching further and serving better.
